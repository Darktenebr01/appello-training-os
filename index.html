APPELLO Executive Assistant Training OS

APPELLO EA Academy is a free, browser-based executive assistant training simulator for complete beginners. It recreates a calm executive office desktop where learners practise real administrative workflows without needing an account or a real company system.

What you can practise

  • Professional email, reply, forwarding, inbox triage, etiquette, and follow-up.
  • Calendar scheduling, time zones, room booking, conflicts, and meeting invitations.
  • Task prioritization, My Day planning, reminders, and action trackers.
  • Meeting preparation, notes, files, spreadsheets, documents, and an office-style chat workspace.
  • 21 guided scenarios in Learn, Practice, and timed Test modes with hints, scoring, skills, and badges.

The training workstation is an original APPELLO learning tool. It stores progress locally in the browser and is suitable for self-study, classroom demonstrations, and administrative onboarding practice.
